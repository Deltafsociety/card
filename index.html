About

I’m a cybersecurity newbie with just enough confidence to be dangerous and just enough knowledge to realize I’m still dangerous. I live in FLOSS ecosystems like it’s a moral philosophy rather than an operating model, romanticizing open-source communities while silently googling every error message like it’s a life-or-death situation. I try to blend into cybersecurity spaces while still mentally buffering on basic concepts, acting like I’m one exploit away from “understanding systems” when in reality I’m one typo away from breaking my own setup. But hey, I’m interested in the intersection of tech, psychology, philosophy, and sociology, which is a polite way of saying I want to understand both how systems fail and why humans insist on repeating the same mistakes in increasingly digital formats. So yeah, I’m basically exploring the human mind and computer systems at the same time, which is ambitious, slightly chaotic, and statistically guaranteed to produce at least one unnecessary existential crisis per debugging session.
